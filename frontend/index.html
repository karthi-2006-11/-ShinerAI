Research Scope & Guardrails

ShinerAI predicts impending low dissolved oxygen events (< 3.0 mg/L within a 2-hour forecast horizon) based on recent discrete pond telemetry. It does NOT predict fish disease, infections, or fish mortality. The 3.0 mg/L threshold remains a provisional project threshold adopted for this research task and must not be interpreted as universally applicable to all aquatic species or operational environments.

Local SHAP explanations represent mathematical attributions within the trained gradient-boosted decision tree; they do not establish biological causation. All evaluations reflect the specific sensor datasets collected during Phase 1–3.
